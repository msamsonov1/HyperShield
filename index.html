Aero-Structural Dynamics & Phase-Space State Inversion

Hypersonic Inflatable Aerodynamic Decelerators (HIAD) substitute traditional rigid metallic/composite aeroshells with stacked pressurized synthetic toroids (p-phenylene terephthalamide / Kevlar bladders) wrapped in Flexible Thermal Protection Systems (FTPS). Deceleration drag directly stresses axial and radial tension webbing.

1. Drag-to-Webbing Elastic Force Coupling

Total vehicle aerodynamic drag force $D$ is governed by hypersonic dynamic pressure $q_\infty$ and total inflated cross-sectional reference area $S(a)$. By Newton's second law, deceleration acceleration $a = D / m_{\text{veh}}$. In nominal states, total strap tension $F_{\text{web}}$ is a strictly monotonic function of drag:

$$D = \frac{1}{2} \rho_\infty v_\infty^2 C_D(\text{Ma}, \delta_r) S(a) = m_{\text{veh}} \cdot a$$
$$F_{\text{web}, i}(\theta) = \frac{m_{\text{veh}} \cdot a}{N_{\text{straps}} \cos \phi} \left(1 + \frac{4 z_{\text{CoM}}}{D_{\text{flare}}} \tan \alpha \cos(\theta - \theta_0)\right) + k_t \delta_r$$

Where $N_{\text{straps}}$ is the strap count, $\phi$ is the flare cone half-angle ($60^\circ$ for standard NASA HIAD), $\alpha$ is angle of attack, and $\delta_r$ is toroidal pressure-dependent radial flare deflection.

Nominal Phase Relationship: $\partial F_{\text{web}} / \partial a > 0$ strictly throughout hypersonic trajectory.

2. Toroidal Pressure-Deflection Virial State

The inflatable flare consists of stacked concentric toroids with internal gauge pressures $P_k$ (typically 100–150 kPa). Thin-walled torus membrane stress equilibrium relates structural rigidity to internal gas mass $n_k R T_k$:

$$\sigma_{\theta} = \frac{P_k r_{\text{tube}}}{2 t_{\text{wall}}} \left( \frac{2 R_k + r_{\text{tube}} \sin \theta}{R_k + r_{\text{tube}} \sin \theta} \right), \quad \delta_r(P_k) \approx \frac{F_{\text{aero}} R_k^2}{2 \pi E_{\text{kevlar}} I_t + P_k V_k}$$

If a puncture or micro-meteoroid seam rupture occurs, internal mass flow exhausts into vacuum:

$$\dot{m}_{\text{leak}} = C_d A_{\text{hole}} P_k \sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R T_k} \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}$$

Loss of $P_k$ collapses the local structural bending modulus, causing local strap slackening and an instantaneous downward divergence from the expected $(a, F)$ trajectory.

Hazard Signature: High deceleration ($a > 10\,g$) accompanied by abrupt local tension drop ($\Delta F < -35\%$).
